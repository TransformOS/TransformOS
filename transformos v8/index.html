What is TransformOS

Many organisations don't know where they are. Almost none know where that leads.

TransformOS is an integrated transformation system that takes an organisation through eight connected stages, from an evidence-based diagnostic of the business as it stands through to a fully costed financial model. Each stage inherits everything the stages before it produced, so the diagnostic informs the strategy, the strategy shapes the roadmap, and the roadmap underpins the business case and the numbers that sit beneath it. The result is a single, coherent body of work rather than a set of separate exercises that never quite reconcile with one another.

Behind it sits three decades of building and rebuilding business models, and a transformation playbook developed across multiple companies founded, scaled and sold. The frameworks, the sequencing, and the judgement about which findings matter and in what order are what separate TransformOS from a template. This is what makes TransformOS entirely unique, unable to be replicated, and something you, or your organisation, have yet to experience.

At the centre is TOM+, an eleven-dimension target operating model. It extends the eight dimensions of the standard model with the three that matter most to a business that has to fund itself: Commercial Performance, Financial Sustainability, and Innovation & Future Readiness. That is the difference between an operating model that describes an organisation and one that can carry it forward.

01
Where you are
A complete, evidence-based picture of the business as it stands — commercially, operationally, financially. Assessed across eleven dimensions using your own data and filings, alongside full external due diligence. Not a survey. Not an opinion.
02
Where that leads
TransformOS models the trajectory the current model is on — where revenue, margin, capability and valuation end up if nothing changes. Built from data, market trends and strategic insight, not guesswork.
03
How you change it
Then it builds the system to alter the outcome. A 36-month roadmap, a target operating model, a board-ready business case and a full financial model — sequenced, costed and evidenced, so the destination actually changes.
